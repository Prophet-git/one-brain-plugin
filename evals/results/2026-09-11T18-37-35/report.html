  • A run's score is the weighted fraction of its graders that passed; a "perfect" run passed every grader.
  • A case's score is the mean of its runs; a case passes when its score is at or above the 100% threshold (the tick on each case bar).

  • Judge votes are independent samples of the LLM judge; the majority decides pass or fail.
  • No baseline arm was run (ablation off) — this report shows absolute scores only and cannot say whether the plugin caused them. Re-run with --ablation with-without to measure the plugin’s effect.
